# PoP 2.0 product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

adaptive

This repository delivers a Flutter Android application and Flutter web portal, with platform-appropriate composition rather than a desktop dashboard squeezed into a phone.

## Users

Women participating in rural Self-Help Groups, Smart Sakhis supporting meetings and enterprises, and field teams use the mobile app. Programme managers and administrators use the web portal. The RFP specifically describes limited digital literacy, varied reading/writing ability and unreliable connectivity.

## Product Purpose

Digitise SHG records and enterprise operations while preserving transparency, financial accountability and continuity of work offline. Programme teams need a separate monitoring/reporting workspace.

## Operating Context

Source: RfpDocuments_6a117cf26110482d92618ed039e0786d.pdf, sections2,2.1,2.2,4,5 and6.3. The original demo scope and synthetic fixtures are preserved in PoP_2_Demo_Coding_Pack. Mobile tasks are meetings, attendance, savings, principal repayment, enterprise entries, registration and support. Essential information and committed entries remain available offline; visible pending/accepted/failure states matter.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Flutter mobile/web, Node.js/TypeScript API, PostgreSQL and native encrypted Drift storage are established. Preserve working permissions, integer-paise arithmetic, frozen operation identities, offline persistence, once-only synchronization, financial correction history and role separation. Never invent programme metrics, historical trends, session identity or financial totals.

The RFP's full language requirement is English, Hindi, Odia, Ho and Santhali, plus voice-assisted navigation/audio instructions. This proposal demo currently implements English/Hindi with bundled fonts and help. Other languages, reviewed audio and live AI/official WhatsApp remain unavailable/planned; show their actual states. The local chat simulator creates real synthetic support records and sends no external messages. No approved final BRD/wireframes or TUIF brand assets were supplied. This redesign is for proposal review, not a claim of TUIF sign-off or production acceptance.

## Brand Commitments

Product name PoP2.0; Trickle Up India Foundation is project context. Keep fictional-data disclosure legible. The user explicitly requested a substantial app/web redesign and delegated design direction. The earlier demo aesthetic is replaceable; functionality and RFP inclusion requirements remain authoritative.

## Evidence on Hand

Synthetic fixture assets, functioning API and clients, tests, recordings and screenshots are in the repository. No real participant images, approved logo, speech assets or new historical analytics exist. Use initials and consistent labelled icons; decorative content must not imply unavailable functionality.

## Product Principles

- Make the next task obvious, particularly during a meeting.
- Treat pending money separately from confirmed money.
- Prefer selection and presets to typing; preserve input and provide understandable recovery.
- Adapt information density to the role and device.
- Make product capability and connection states truthful.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use clear icons with text, large touch targets, high contrast, simple language, sensible focus order and text scaling. Maintain English/Hindi coverage and offline font delivery. Actual TalkBack speech and the full cached-start target remain documented acceptance gaps; visual redesign must not conceal them.
