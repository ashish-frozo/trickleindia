# PoP2.0 app and portal redesign

User authorization: requested a substantial redesign of both platforms, delegated the design direction, then explicitly required the RFP to guide UI decisions.

## Direction

A refined programme workspace: clear financial records, human task context and precise operational structure. The inspiration is a well-designed programme report brought into a native field tool, not a wall of generic rounded dashboard tiles. Deep forest anchors navigation and the meeting/passbook focus; pale warm surfaces support daylight use; sage and ochre distinguish supporting context and pending work.

The seven grounded systems considered were an SHG ledger, meeting agenda, civic wayfinding, field-work kit, transit operations board, mobile financial workspace, and programme report spread. Direction7 was assigned by the design seed28d8fe24 and is compatible with the user's delegated brief. Spectrogram, travel-wallet and specimen/catalog challengers were rejected because they reduce audience identification or task clarity for the RFP's users. The result keeps native affordances and clear typography rather than carrying those metaphors into controls.

## Mobile

Compact brand/language header and a legible fictional-data disclosure. Home: greeting/date, a strong meeting panel, a concise sync row, assigned-group summary and actionable follow-up. Passbook: identity and confirmed balance together, a separate amber pending strip, compact principal summary, dated activity and one record action. Shared inputs, preset chips, review dialogs and status rows receive the same visual system.

## Portal

Forest full-height navigation with a recognisable text wordmark and authenticated identity. Clean top toolbar, a strong page title, a compact group filter, connected financial summary band, proper group comparison rows/table, and separate activity/support areas. Use actual dataset composition; no invented charts, growth rates or provider capabilities. At tablet/mobile widths the structure adapts and tables become labelled rows.

## Constraints and verification

Preserve all controller/store/API behavior, integer money, role scope, frozen operations, receipt reconciliation and form commitment. Keep mobile text/touch accessibility and complete English/Hindi resources. Bundle fonts. Follow Material affordances and safe areas. Review desktop/phone together in one screenshot pass, batch fixes, then confirm once. Record final design truth and reviewer verdict. RFP traceability is in RFP_UI_REQUIREMENTS.md.
