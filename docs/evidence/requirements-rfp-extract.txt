0009 1. About Trickle Up India Foundation (TUIF)
0010
0011 Trickle Up India Foundation (TUIF) is a nationally registered non-profit organization that partners with
0012 women in ultra-poverty in India to build economic opportunity and drive inclusion. In collaboration
0013 with government agencies and local non-governmental organizations, TUIF reaches underserved
0014 women in remote rural areas through programs that enable them to build resilient pathways out of
0015 poverty for themselves, their families, and their communities.
0016 Since 2013, TUIF has been working with women in ultra-poverty who have faced lifelong
0017 discrimination and socio-economic exclusion. Through its programs, participants develop sustainable
0018 livelihoods and strong savings habits, strengthening their capacity, self-reliance, and stability. This
0019 process fosters improved self-esteem and resilience, with positive ripple effects across families and
0020 communities over generations.
0021 TUIF has reached approximately 150,000 extremely poor women and their families across 22 districts
0022 in Jharkhand, Odisha, and West Bengal.
0023 Its programs are grounded in rigorous evidence, with a strong focus on the Graduation Approach. This
0024 sequenced and time-bound model combines participant identification, livelihood planning based on
0025 market assessments, training and coaching, consumption support, asset transfer, and savings support.
0026 The approach promotes both economic and social inclusion by integrating participants into savings
0027 groups.

0029 2. PoP 2.0 application:
0030
0031 PoP 2.0 (Package of Practices 2.0) is envisioned as a comprehensive AI-enabled Digital Enterprise
0032 Management Platform designed to strengthen women's economic empowerment through digitization
0033 of Self-Help Groups (SHGs), Micro Enterprise Groups (MEGs), enterprise operations, cluster
0034 management, market linkages, and financial governance.
0035
0036 PoP 2.0 is designed as a simple and user-friendly digital platform that can be used even in areas with
0037 limited internet connectivity. The application should continue to function during network
0038 interruptions and automatically synchronize information when internet connectivity becomes
0039 available. The application will prioritize ease of use through local language support, voice-assisted
0040 navigation, minimal data entry, and simple user interfaces, while providing advanced monitoring and
0041 analytics through a separate web portal for programme teams.
0042
0043 The solution will consist of a mobile application for SHG members, Smart Sakhis and field teams, along
0044 with a web portal for programme managers and administrators. The mobile application will focus on
0045 simple day-to-day operations, while the web portal will provide advanced monitoring, reporting,
0046 analytics, content management and administrative functions.
0047
0048 2.1 Offline Functionality and Data Synchronisation
0049 Considering the limited internet connectivity in the project's operational areas, the application must
0050 support offline data entry and access to essential functionalities. Users should be able to enter, save
0051 and access relevant information without an active internet connection. The application shall
0052
0053 automatically synchronise locally stored data with the central server whenever internet connectivity
0054 is restored.
0055 The system should incorporate the following capabilities:
0056 • Enable users to enter, save and access relevant data while offline.
0057 • Automatically synchronise locally stored data with the central database when internet
0058      connectivity is restored.
0059 • Identify failed or pending synchronisation and provide appropriate status notifications to users.
0060 • Prevent duplicate data submissions during synchronisation.
0061 • Ensure data consistency and appropriate conflict resolution when multiple users or devices update
0062      records.
0063 • Provide users with visibility of synchronisation status, including pending, successful and failed
0064      submissions.
0065 The agency shall propose and implement an appropriate data synchronisation mechanism to ensure
0066 data integrity, reliability and continuity of application usage in low-connectivity environments.
0067
0068 2.2 Language Support and Accessibility
0069 The application shall be designed to ensure accessibility and ease of use for women with limited digital
0070 literacy and varying levels of familiarity with smartphones. The user interface and navigation should
0071 be simple, intuitive and suitable for users from diverse linguistic backgrounds.
0072 The application shall support the following languages:
0073      • English
0074      • Hindi
0075      • Odia
0076      • Ho
0077      • Santhali
0078 The agency shall ensure the following accessibility features:
0079      • Provide multilingual user interfaces, including appropriate language selection options.
0080      • Incorporate voice-assisted navigation and audio instructions to guide users through key
0081          application functions.
0082      • Use simple language, clear icons, visual cues and intuitive navigation.
0083      • Minimise manual data entry through dropdowns, predefined options and other simplified
0084          input mechanisms wherever feasible.
0085      • Ensure consistency in language presentation across the mobile application and relevant user-
0086          facing features.
0087      • Design interfaces that are accessible and easy to operate for users with limited reading and
0088          writing abilities.
0089 The agency shall demonstrate the proposed language and accessibility features during the design and
0090 User Acceptance Testing (UAT) stages.
